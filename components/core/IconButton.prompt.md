An icon-only action; also the tinted glyph tile at the top of feature cards.

```jsx
<IconButton label="Zoom in" variant="outline"><Icon name="plus" /></IconButton>
<IconButton label="Layers" variant="soft"><Icon name="layers" size={20} /></IconButton>
<IconButton label="Ukur" variant="soft-accent" shape="circle"><Icon name="ruler" /></IconButton>
```

Sizes are 32/38/44px. Use 44px for anything touch-facing. Rounded, not pill, unless `shape="circle"`.
