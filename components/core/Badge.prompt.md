A status marker in uppercase mono — reads as a data label, not a sticker.

```jsx
<Badge>Survei</Badge>
<Badge tone="accent" dot>Berjalan</Badge>
<Badge tone="neutral" size="sm">Arsip</Badge>
<Badge tone="solid">Baru</Badge>
```

One or two words maximum. For multi-word, softer labels (topics, filters) use Tag instead.
